Hi, I'm Fran Ortiz, a Product Engineer. I like being involved in the whole life of a product: understanding what users
need, deciding what to build, shipping it, and then running and improving it.

I see product development as a continuous learning process. I work in small steps and use feedback from users, from the
system and from the team to decide what to do next.

During my career, I have had the opportunity to build different products, trying to provide value by implementing
continuous learning cultures based on Extreme Programming (XP) and DevOps.

I choose tools based on the problem to solve. Technology is a means to meet users' needs, not the goal. Over the years
I've worked with different languages, distributed systems, data pipelines and, more recently, AI agents.

### What you'll find here

Notes from that work. Some posts are about product discovery: how to **build the right thing**. Others are about
product delivery: how to **build the thing right**.
